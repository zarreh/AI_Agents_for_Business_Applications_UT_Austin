LOGISTICS POLICY RULEBOOK
Internal Document: Supply Chain Operations

POL-001: Weather Disruption Protocol
Scope: Applies when a delivery status indicates a delay where adverse weather conditions are
identified as the primary cause by carrier reports or meteorological alerts.
Standard Action: Reroute
Decision Matrix:
- Delay < 3 days: Monitor status and hold shipment at the current hub. Do not initiate rerouting.
- Delay 3-5 days: Initiate reroute via alternate regional hub if route availability exceeds 80%.
- Delay > 5 days: Immediate escalation to the senior dispatch department for direct carrier negotiation.

Geographic Exceptions:
- Western Europe: Mandatory reroute via Frankfurt Hub.
- North America: Mandatory reroute via Chicago Distribution Center.
- South America: Hold at São Paulo Hub and initiate customer delay notification.

POL-002: Standard Late Delivery Protocol
Scope: Triggered when a high risk of late delivery is identified, and the projected delay is
between 2 and 5 days.
Standard Action: Reroute
Operational Rules:
1. Perform a regional lookup for alternate carrier routes.
2. If an alternate exists: Initiate reroute and issue formal carrier notification.
3. If no alternate: Change status to "Hold" and upgrade shipping mode to "Expedited."
4. High-Value Clause: Any shipment with a sales value exceeding $3,000 bypasses standard
   checks and is automatically upgraded to "Expedited" shipping.

POL-003: Critical Escalation Policy
Scope: Triggered by terminal status errors, shipping cancellations, or extreme delays.
Standard Action: Management Intervention Required
Escalation Criteria:
- Delivery Status is "Shipping canceled."
- Total delay exceeds 5 calendar days.
- Sales value > $3,000 AND high late-delivery risk.
- Corporate customer SLA breach is imminent (less than 4 hours to breach).

Response Time SLAs:
- Corporate Customers: Resolution or status update required within 30 minutes.
- Consumer Customers: Resolution or status update required within 120 minutes.

POL-004: Shipping Mode SLA Commitments
Scope: Defines the threshold for service level agreement (SLA) breaches based on the
customer's selected shipping tier.
Calculation Logic: Delay Gap = (Projected Delivery Date - Scheduled Delivery Date)

SLA Tier Matrix:
- Same Day: Scheduled 1 day, Tolerance 0 days, Action on Breach: Expedite
- First Class: Scheduled 2 days, Tolerance 1 day, Action on Breach: Expedite
- Second Class: Scheduled 4 days, Tolerance 2 days, Action on Breach: Reroute
- Standard Class: Scheduled 7 days, Tolerance 3 days, Action on Breach: Hold

Proactive Monitoring: Shipments within 1 day of their specific tolerance threshold must be
flagged for immediate priority review by the dispatch team.

POL-005: Customer Segment Priority Matrix
Scope: Determines urgency and resource allocation during routing conflicts, limited carrier
capacity, or regional disruptions.

Priority Levels:
- Corporate: Priority High, Escalation Window 30 Minutes
- Home Office: Priority Medium, Escalation Window 90 Minutes
- Consumer: Priority Medium, Escalation Window 120 Minutes

Operational Rules:
1. Corporate customers receive first-priority for limited alternate route capacity and container space.
2. Consumer orders strictly follow the Standard Late Delivery Protocol (POL-002).
3. Home Office orders are managed under Consumer protocols unless the total order value
   exceeds $2,000, at which point they inherit Corporate priority status.

POL-006: Special Cargo Handling Protocols
Scope: Overrides standard routing procedures based on specific product category requirements
or environmental sensitivities.

Category Constraints:
- Electronics: Requires temperature-controlled routing. Initiate reroute immediately if the
  delay exceeds 2 days to protect sensitive components.
- Furniture: Bulk handling priority. Delays up to 5 days are tolerable; rerouting should be
  avoided due to significant transit and oversized handling costs.
- Clothing: Seasonal sensitivity. If delivery is within 7 days of a seasonal transition
  (e.g., holiday periods), upgrade to Expedited.
- Sports: Follow standard handling procedures.
- Books: Lowest priority. Use as "filler" capacity on alternate routes where weight limits allow.
